Calculator FAQ

Are these calculators accurate?

They use standard amortization and APR formulas. Results are estimates; lenders may calculate fees, day counts and rounding differently.

What's the difference between APR and interest rate?

The interest rate is the cost of borrowing the principal. APR adds mandatory fees such as origination fees, so it reflects the true yearly cost.

What is EMI?

Equated Monthly Instalment: the fixed monthly payment, a term widely used in India. It's the same as the monthly payment in our Payment/EMI calculator.
