Supporter FAQ

Is my contribution tax-deductible?

BiLoans is not a registered charity, so contributions are generally not tax-deductible. Your receipt will state this clearly.

How do I pay?

Use a payment button above if shown, or submit the pledge form and we'll email secure payment options (card, PayPal, bank transfer).

Can I cancel a monthly contribution?

Yes, anytime. Just reply to any receipt or use the contact page.

Do sponsors influence your content?

No. Donations and sponsorships never affect our editorial ratings.
